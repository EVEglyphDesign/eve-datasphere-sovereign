SYSRC

14 fields · 2 key · 1 foreign keys · sourced from EVEglyphDesign canonical structure. The register itself is the governance artifact.

System of record registry. Every source the interaction journal mirrors from: a Salesforce org, a ServiceNow instance, a telephony platform, a satellite ground segment, a parish register. Records what is read, under whose authority, how often, and on what legal basis — because a mirror without a declared basis is surveillance.
