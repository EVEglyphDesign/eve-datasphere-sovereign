MM — Materials Management

MLGN

26 fields · 3 key · 17 foreign keys · sourced from leanx.eu

Material Data for Each Warehouse Number — the warehouse-level (WM) master data for a material, one row per material per warehouse number, carrying the storage-strategy defaults (put-away/picking storage-type indicators, loading-equipment quantities and units, storage-unit types, bulk-storage and 2-step-picking flags) that classic Warehouse Management uses to place and remove stock. Paired with MLGT for the storage-type-specific bin assignment; together they are the WM-material lane that sits alongside MARD's IM-level stock quantities.
