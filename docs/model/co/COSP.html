CO — Controlling

COSP

151 fields · 10 key · 19 foreign keys · sourced from leanx.eu

CO Object: Cost Totals for External Postings — period-bucketed totals (16 posting periods per row) for CO postings originating outside Controlling (FI journal entries, MM goods movements, invoices) against any CO object. The aggregate counterpart to the COEP line-item table for externally sourced costs.
