EFFSTD

9 fields · 2 key · 1 foreign keys · sourced from EVEglyphDesign extension. Standard codes are references to their publishers, not reproductions.

Reporting standard datapoint registry. Maps an effect category to the published disclosure point it answers — a GRI, ESRS, ISSB or SASB code — so a community's own measurement can be reported outward in the language a regulator or auditor already reads, without the community having to adopt that vocabulary internally.
