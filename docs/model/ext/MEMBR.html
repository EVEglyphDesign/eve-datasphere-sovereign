MEMBR

17 fields · 2 key · 2 foreign keys · sourced from EVEglyphDesign extension. Consent-scoped: a member controls the visibility of its own record.

Member organisation registry. The professional community this model exists to serve: the firms, parishes, councils, schools and non-profits that post effects, verify each other's claims and hold recognition. Membership carries a peer group and a standing, because the point of the register is that an organisation which accomplished something can be told apart from one that described something.
