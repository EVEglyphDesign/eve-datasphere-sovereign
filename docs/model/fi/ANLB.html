FI — Financial Accounting

ANLB

49 fields · 5 key · 12 foreign keys · sourced from leanx.eu

Depreciation Terms — depreciation-area-level control record for a fixed asset: depreciation key, useful life, start dates, and scrap value, one row per asset per depreciation area per validity period. Anchors to the ANLA asset master via BUKRS/ANLN1/ANLN2 and drives the depreciation postings that land in ACDOCA/ANEP; if the depreciation key or useful life here drifts from what last posted to ACDOCA, planned and posted depreciation diverge and the asset subledger no longer reconciles to the universal journal.
