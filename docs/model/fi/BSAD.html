BSAD

52 fields · 9 key · 6 foreign keys · sourced from leanx.eu

Accounting: Secondary Index for Customers (Cleared Items) — same row shape as BSID, but for items that have already been cleared (AUGDT/AUGBL populated). This is the destination an open item on the dealer's accounting schedule moves to the moment a payment or credit is applied — the clearing event a schedule-to-GL reconciliation has to account for, not just the open balance.
