SD — Sales & Distribution

VBKD

42 fields · 3 key · 22 foreign keys · sourced from leanx.eu

Sales document business data — header- and item-level commercial terms (price group, Incoterms, payment terms, exchange rates for pricing, billing plan reference, customer PO reference) that condition the pricing and billing of a sales document. Can occur once per document (header, POSNR = '000000') or per item where item-specific business data overrides the header. SAP's real table carries roughly 130 fields, many industry-specific (IS-OIL, tax, SEPA); this definition is scoped to the key, pricing/currency, payment, and reference fields the SD pricing and billing model consumes — never silently truncated below that scope.
