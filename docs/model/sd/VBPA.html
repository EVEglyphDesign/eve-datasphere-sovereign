SD — Sales & Distribution

VBPA

24 fields · 4 key · 9 foreign keys · sourced from leanx.eu

Sales document partner — the partner-function table that answers who the sold-to, ship-to, payer, and bill-to actually were on a given sales document (header, POSNR = '000000') or item, each row keyed by PARVW (partner function, e.g. AG sold-to, WE ship-to, RE bill-to, RG payer) and resolving to a customer (KUNNR) or vendor (LIFNR).
