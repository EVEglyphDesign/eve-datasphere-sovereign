KONH

22 fields · 2 key · 11 foreign keys · sourced from leanx.eu

Condition header — the pricing master record header behind every condition record (rebate agreement, price list, promotion). Grouped with KONP (condition items/rates) as the master-data pair that condition-access tables such as A004 point to via KNUMH; document-level pricing captured in KONV during order/delivery/billing processing is a runtime snapshot of what KONH/KONP defined at the time of pricing.
