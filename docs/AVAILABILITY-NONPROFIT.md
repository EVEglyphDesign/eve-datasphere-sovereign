# Availability — EVE DICOE Blueprint

**Standing offer, effective 2026-07-31.**

**EVE DICOE Blueprint designs are available upon request and without charge to qualified
non-profit organizations.**

No licence fee. No seat count. No term. A qualified non-profit asks, and receives the
blueprint.

## What is being offered

This repository is the **wireframe** — the field-level structures of a customer-owned mirror
of the enterprise systems of record. The **blueprint** is what is extracted from it: the
design a following organization uses to stand up its own sovereign data surface without
re-deriving any of this work. The wireframe is public to everyone. The blueprint is provided
on request, at no charge, to organizations that qualify.

## Who qualifies

Registered non-profit and charitable organizations, and the parishes, dioceses, councils,
schools, co-operatives and mutual-aid bodies operating under them. Qualification is
established by the organization's own registration; no application process is imposed on a
body that already answers to a regulator.

## Terms

- **Without charge** means without charge — not a trial, not a discount, not a credit against
  future work.
- The blueprint is provided under the same doctrine as the model: mirror, never cannibalise.
  A recipient inherits the non-proliferation compact along with the design.
- Attribution to EVEglyphDesign is requested and not enforced.
- Commercial and for-profit use is a separate conversation and is not covered by this notice.

## How to request

Open an issue on
[the repository](https://github.com/EVEglyphDesign/eve-datasphere-sovereign/issues) naming the
organization and the surface being built.
